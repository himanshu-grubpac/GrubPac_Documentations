1. Executive Summary

What is ERPNext?

ERPNext is an open-source ERP system built on the Frappe Framework. The core covers accounting, inventory, purchasing, sales, project management, asset tracking, and manufacturing. Some modules like HR/Payroll and advanced Helpdesk have been split into separate Frappe apps that need to be installed alongside ERPNext. Widely adopted across small and mid-size businesses globally.

What is Frappe Framework?

Frappe is the full-stack Python web framework that ERPNext runs on. The key idea: every business entity (invoice, asset, ticket) is a "DocType" — a structured document. When you define a DocType, Frappe generates the database table, REST API, form UI, list view, and permission layer automatically. It also includes a workflow engine, background job scheduler, email integration, and a basic front-end. Think of it as Django + admin panel + workflow engine in one package.

Where ERPNext Fits

Asset lifecycle tracking (every box from procurement to retirement), inventory management (boxes, battery packs, solar panels, compressor units, camera modules, accessories), OEM purchase workflows with multi-currency, quality inspection on incoming shipments, customer CRM and sales pipeline, helpdesk and support tickets, maintenance scheduling, accounting with regional tax compliance, approval workflows, role-based access control, reporting dashboards, and audit logging. HR/Payroll available as a separate Frappe app on the same instance.
Recommendation: Use ERPNext for internal enterprise operations — procurement, inventory, assets, sales, accounting. Install the HRMS app for HR/Payroll when needed. The Frappe architectural patterns (DocTypes, workflows, RBAC) are worth studying for any future custom enterprise platform.

Asset Management High Priority

What it is: Track physical assets from procurement to retirement with complete lifecycle history, depreciation, warranty, and maintenance records.

How ERPNext provides it: Asset DocType with purchase date, location, custodian, warranty, depreciation schedule, status, and linked maintenance. Supports serial numbers and batch tracking.

How GrubPac Uses This

Example Workflow

  • → Box GP-2026-001 arrives from OEM vendor → Purchase Receipt created
  • → QA inspects compressor, temperature zones, solar panel, battery, 4G/IoT → Quality Inspection logged
  • → Box passes QA → Asset record created with serial number, warranty dates, firmware version
  • → Deployed to customer site → Asset Movement logged

  • → 3 years later: unit retired → Asset scrapped, replacement dispatched
Recommendation: This is the most important module for GrubPac. Every smart box unit should be a tracked asset with full history — procurement date, QA results, customer deployment, maintenance records, and eventual retirement.

Sales Management High Priority

What it is: Quotation → Sales Order → Delivery Note → Sales Invoice chain with pricing rules, discounts, and subscription billing.

How ERPNext provides it: Full sales workflow with multi-currency, tax templates, and delivery tracking. ERPNext has a basic Subscription DocType that auto-generates invoices on a schedule — it works for simple recurring billing but is not a full subscription platform. For complex billing (usage-based, tiered pricing, dunning), you would need external tools.

GrubPac Sales Scenarios

  • → Hardware sale: Quotation for 200 GrubPac units to fleet operator → Sales Order → Delivery → Invoice
  • → Subscription: Monthly cloud dashboard, firmware updates, IoT monitoring per unit
  • → GrubMedia revenue: Ad monetization via LED display panel — track per-unit ad revenue
  • → AMC contract: Annual maintenance contract with compressor and battery service SLAs
  • → Renewal: Auto-generated renewal quotations before contract expiry
Recommendation: Good fit for hardware sales and basic recurring invoicing (per-unit subscription for cloud/monitoring). The subscription feature handles simple per-device monthly billing. For complex pricing (usage-based, GrubMedia ad revenue splits), consider integrating with a dedicated billing tool via API later.

Subcontracting Medium Priority

What it is: Manage outsourced manufacturing — track materials sent to vendor, finished goods received, quality inspection on receipt.

GrubPac use: OEM manufacturing workflow — vendor builds GrubPac smart delivery boxes to spec.

Human Resources Medium Priority

What it is: Employee lifecycle — onboarding, attendance, leave management, training, appraisals, offboarding.

How ERPNext provides it: HR is no longer part of ERPNext core. Since v14, it lives in a separate app called Frappe HRMS (frappe/hrms). It installs on the same Frappe instance and integrates with ERPNext accounting. Includes Employee master, Leave Application, Attendance, Training, and Appraisals.

GrubPac use: Manage engineering, operations, support, and sales teams. Track attendance, leave balances, recruitment pipeline.

Note: Requires installing the HRMS app separately (bench get-app hrms). Worth setting up once the team is large enough that spreadsheet-based HR becomes a pain — roughly 15-20+ people.

Payroll Medium Priority

What it is: Salary structure, payroll processing, tax deductions, reimbursements, payslip generation.

How ERPNext provides it: Payroll is part of the same Frappe HRMS app — not ERPNext core. Includes Salary Structure, Payroll Entry for batch processing, Salary Slip generation, and auto-posting journal entries to ERPNext accounting.

GrubPac use: Process monthly salaries, manage reimbursement claims. Regional payroll compliance varies — some countries have mature support, others may need configuration.

Note: Comes with the HRMS app. Payroll compliance varies by region — some have mature built-in support, others may need custom setup or third-party integration.

Accounting High Priority

What it is: Double-entry accounting with chart of accounts, journal entries, invoicing, expense claims, tax management.

How ERPNext provides it: Full GL accounting, Purchase/Sales Invoice auto-posting, Payment Entry, Bank Reconciliation, Financial Statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow).

GrubPac use: Track all revenue (hardware + subscriptions + AMC), expenses (OEM purchases, operations), regional tax compliance.

Recommendation: This replaces the need for separate accounting software. Purchase and sales transactions automatically create GL entries, so you don't double-enter data. Needs a proper Chart of Accounts setup upfront — worth spending time on this during initial configuration.

GrubPac use: Track payments to OEM vendors, customer receipts in local currencies, budget allocation per department.

REST APIs / Integrations Medium Priority

What it is: Every DocType automatically gets CRUD REST APIs. Plus webhook support, OAuth, and API key authentication.

How ERPNext provides it: Auto-generated endpoints: /api/resource/{DocType} for all operations. Webhooks for external event triggers.

GrubPac use: External billing systems can pull invoice data. Customer portals can create tickets via API. IoT dashboards can update asset status.

Recommendation: Useful for connecting ERPNext to external systems. The auto-generated API means you don't need to build custom endpoints — just authenticate and call the standard resource URLs. Worth testing the API early to understand rate limits and response formats.

Document Management Medium Priority

What it is: File attachments linked to any business record — contracts, reports, images stored alongside transactions.

How ERPNext provides it: File manager with drag-and-drop. Attach files to any DocType record. S3-compatible storage backend.

GrubPac use: Contracts on Customer records, QA photos on inspections, shipment docs on Purchase Receipts, warranty certificates on Assets.

Recommendation: Works for attaching files to records. Not a replacement for a full DMS (no versioning, no folder structure), but good enough for contracts, QA photos, and shipment documents linked to specific transactions.

Audit Logs / Activity Tracking High Priority

What it is: Complete change history for every record — who changed what, when, before/after values.

How ERPNext provides it: Version tracking on every DocType with field-level change logs. Activity log for user actions. Access log for document views.

GrubPac use: Full traceability for enterprise and compliance-sensitive customers — every asset movement, ticket change, and financial transaction permanently logged.

Recommendation: This is table stakes for compliance-sensitive customers (pharma cold chain, large enterprise fleets). Version tracking is enabled by default on most DocTypes — verify it is turned on for Asset, Issue, and financial documents during setup.

Scheduler / Background Automation Medium Priority

What it is: Cron-like scheduled jobs, background task queue, and event-driven automation.

How ERPNext provides it: Scheduler hooks (daily, weekly, monthly, hourly, cron). Background workers via Redis queue. Auto Repeat for recurring documents.

GrubPac use: Auto-generate monthly invoices, send maintenance reminders 7 days before service, close stale tickets after 30 days.

Recommendation: The scheduler runs through the background worker containers. Use it for things like auto-generating monthly subscription invoices, sending maintenance reminder emails, or cleaning up stale draft documents.

GrubPac use: Low priority — most customers interact through account managers. Evaluate when customer base grows.

Document-Driven Architecture High Priority

What it is: The foundational design pattern of Frappe/ERPNext. Every business entity is a "DocType" — a structured document with fields, validations, permissions, workflows, and auto-generated APIs. This is how the entire system thinks.

Why it matters: Instead of building separate schemas, API routes, and permission logic for each entity, you define a DocType and Frappe auto-generates everything: database table, REST API, form UI, list view, print format, workflow support, and audit trail.
