1. Executive Summary

Why MapmyIndia?

For Indian road networks, global providers (Google, HERE) struggle with dynamic traffic rules, physical dividers, and local one-way streets. MapmyIndia leverages physical ground surveyors, a direct partnership with ISRO (NavIC satellite system), and IoT telemetry from millions of trucks to provide the highest-accuracy ground-truth data available in the subcontinent.

Business Objective

To implement enterprise-grade driver monitoring and safety compliance. Specifically, analyzing noisy GPS telemetry directly from the GrubPac IoT boxes in real-time to detect dangerous violations such as wrong-side driving, speeding, and unauthorized route deviations.
Recommendation: Utilize MapmyIndia's Map Matching API to convert raw GPS trajectories into verified road segments with direction metadata, acting as the foundation for our internal safety alert system.

Map Matching & Snapping Core API

What it is: Raw GPS coordinates from the IoT box are noisy and "bounce" around. Map Matching takes a sequence of raw GPS coordinates, analyzes the trajectory, and "snaps" them to the exact logical road center-line in the digital database.

The Process

  • → GrubPac Box (IoT Module) sends batch of GPS pings (Lat, Long, Speed, Bearing) every 10 seconds.
  • → GrubPac Backend forwards the batch to MapmyIndia API.
  • → API applies Hidden Markov Models (HMM) to filter out noise and matches the points to physical road links.
  • → API returns the matched coordinates AND the exact Road Segment ID with metadata.

Wrong-Way Detection Logic High Priority

How it works: Wrong-way driving cannot be detected by raw GPS alone. It requires comparing the vehicle's heading against the legally allowed direction of the snapped road segment.

Crucial Requirement: The map database MUST know that a road has a physical divider (splitting it into two one-way roads). MapmyIndia's physical surveying guarantees this metadata exists, whereas global maps often group divided highways into a single two-way line, causing false negatives.

Integration with Uber H3 Geospatial Indexing

What it is: Uber H3 divides the world into hexagonal grids. It is excellent for indexing areas, calculating density, and doing fast proximity searches (e.g., "find all drivers in this 1km hex").

How H3 and MapmyIndia Work Together

  • 1. H3 for Discovery: Use H3 in your database (like PostgreSQL/PostGIS) to quickly index where the driver is and group them by region.
  • 2. MapmyIndia for Rules: H3 knows where you are, but it does NOT know what a road is or what the traffic laws are. Once H3 finds the driver, you pass their exact coordinates to MapmyIndia to check road compliance.
  • 3. The Synergy: "Find all drivers in Hexagon 8a2a1072b59ffff (H3). Take their last 10 GPS pings and check if they are driving the wrong way (MapmyIndia)."
